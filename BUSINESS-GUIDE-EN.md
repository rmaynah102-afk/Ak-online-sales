# Retail & Wholesale Business Operations Handbook

## 1. Set up the business
Define your target customers, core categories and service area. Separate shop cash from personal money. Record opening cash, sales, purchases, expenses, credit and closing cash daily.

## 2. Inventory management
Assign each item a unique SKU/barcode. Record name, category, unit, cost, retail/wholesale prices and quantity. Classify items as fast-moving, slow-moving or dead stock. Set reorder levels and verify quantities, quality and invoices on delivery.

## 3. Pricing and profit
Include freight, packaging, payment fees and expected wastage in landed cost. Gross profit = sales minus cost of goods sold. Net profit = gross profit minus operating expenses. Gross margin % = (selling price − cost) ÷ selling price × 100. Do not copy competitor prices without checking your own cost.

## 4. Cash flow
Profit is not the same as cash in hand. Credit sales may not have been collected yet. Reserve money for replenishment, rent, wages, utilities, transport and emergencies. Reconcile cash every day.

## 5. Credit control
Set a credit limit and due date for each customer. Review overdue balances before extending more credit. Issue a receipt for every collection and confirm the remaining balance.

## 6. Suppliers and purchasing
Compare at least two or three suppliers on total cost, quality, warranty, return terms and delivery time. Match purchase orders, received quantities, invoices and payments.

## 7. Customer service
Explain specifications, warranty and return conditions clearly. Do not use customer contact details for marketing without permission. Set discounts and wholesale tiers only when margins remain sustainable.

## 8. Digital marketing
Use clear product photos and include name, model, price, availability and contact details. Share through Viber, Facebook and TikTok according to platform rules. Track inquiries, orders and revenue from each campaign.

## 9. Staff and security
Separate cashier, stockkeeper and manager duties. Log refunds, discounts and stock adjustments. Never share passwords. Revoke access when staff leave.

## 10. Weekly and monthly checks
- Reconcile cash against records.
- Count physical inventory and compare it with the app.
- Review overdue receivables and payables.
- Review profit, expenses and purchasing trends.
- Identify dead stock and items to reorder.
- Compare supplier prices and quality.
- Verify backups and account security.

## 11. Scale carefully
Add branches, staff, barcode scanners, receipt printers, cloud backup and dashboards in stages. Test every device with the actual phone and app before buying at scale. Never promise an untested feature.
